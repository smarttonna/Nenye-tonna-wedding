Important Guest Guide

✦ Strictly by Invitation Policy

To maintain an intimate and secure celebration, entry to both the ceremony and reception is strictly by invitation card. Each invitation admits one guest. Please bring your physical card or digital pass.

✦ Adults-Only Event (No Kids Please)

As indicated on our invitation card, we respectfully request no children at the celebration. We love your little ones, but hope you understand our wish for an adults-only celebration.

✦ Punctuality & Seating

The ceremony commences promptly at 12:00 PM. We kindly ask our guests to arrive and be comfortably seated by 11:30 AM to ensure smooth proceedings.
